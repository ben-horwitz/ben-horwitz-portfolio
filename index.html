About

Mechanical Engineer

Ben Horwitz

Hi — my name is Ben Horwitz! I am a mechanical engineer by trade and a senior at Middlebury College. I built my technical background through a year of mechanical engineering coursework at Dartmouth, a research position at Georgia Tech under Mechanical Engineering Chair Dr. Carolyn Seepersad, a manufacturing internship at a machine shop, and research at the Center for Additive Manufacturing at St. Louis University. Below are just a few projects I've built: a high-viscosity SLA resin printer, a custom golf putter, and much more. Explore my site to take a closer look at my work!
