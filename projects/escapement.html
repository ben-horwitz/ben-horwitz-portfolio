Mechanism Design

Clock Escapement

A mechanical clock escapement, designed to convert steady rotational energy into a precise, periodic tick — refined over several prototype iterations.

Mechanism Design Precision Machining Iterative Prototyping

The challenge with any escapement is getting the pallet and escape wheel geometry precise enough that the mechanism releases at a consistent, repeatable interval instead of drifting or skipping teeth.

This version went through multiple iterations — adjusting tooth geometry, pallet engagement, and pivot friction — before settling into a version that holds a clean, steady one-second tick.
