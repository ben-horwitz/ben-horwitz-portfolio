Mechanism Design

Quick-Release Mechanism

A tool-free quick-release latch, modeled as a full CAD assembly and then built and tested for repeated one-handed engage/disengage cycles.

CAD Assembly Mechanism Design

The goal was a latch that locks securely under load but releases instantly with one motion — no tools, no fumbling. The full mechanism was modeled and animated as a CAD assembly first to check clearances and range of motion before any parts were cut.

Once built, it was tested through repeated attach/release cycles to make sure the lid seats consistently and the latch doesn't walk loose over time.
