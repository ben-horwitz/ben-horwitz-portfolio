Golf Ball Placer

An automated mechanism that loads a golf ball from a hopper and places it precisely, taken from CAD concept to a working prototype.

Automation CAD Prototyping

The mechanism takes a golf ball from a loading position and places it down in a repeatable spot, cycling automatically instead of needing to be reset by hand between shots.

It started as a CAD concept to work out the load/place motion, then came together as a physical prototype to prove the mechanism actually holds, carries, and releases a ball reliably.
