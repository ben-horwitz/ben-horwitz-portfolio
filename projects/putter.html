CAD / CAM / Machining

Custom Machined Putter

A golf putter modeled in CAD, programmed for CNC machining, and cut from solid stock into a finished, playable head.

CAD CAM CNC Milling

The goal was to take a putter from a full CAD model through to a finished, machined part — face milling, weighting, and all the small geometry that affects feel and roll, not just the CAD render.

The head was modeled first, then programmed in CAM to generate toolpaths for the mill. Getting the face flat and the alignment features accurate took a few passes of fixturing and cut planning before the final part came off the machine.
